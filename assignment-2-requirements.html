Course guidance and team plan

Appendix A, pp.6–7: content considerations

Mehdi: external interfaces/information and significant dependencies. David: essential use case, control flow/concurrency, performance and future-change implications where relevant. Setayesh: glossary and naming conventions.

Appendix B, p.8: recommendations

All: rehearse and allow 1–2 questions. Spence/Mehdi/David: explain mechanisms and legends. Dan: rejected modelling alternatives, trade-offs, defensible findings, constructive feedback and actual lessons.

Team decisions

Ten slides, two per member; 12-minute rehearsal plan; one shared evidence package reused for slides and report; page allocations are adjustable targets.

Assignment boundaries

Section 2, p.1 and Section 7, p.5 assign conceptual/concrete comparison, discrepancy rationales and mitigation to Assignment 3.

The brief prohibits GenAI-written report prose. Each member writes and checks their assigned sections and discloses AI assistance as required by the assignment.

Target: Setayesh 2.5 pages, Spence 2, Mehdi 2.5, David 2, Dan 1.5. One shared evidence package feeds both deliverables; no repeated extraction is required.
