Required report content

  • Source snapshot, scope, research questions, and subsystem claim.
  • Reproducible Understand extraction and grouping method.
  • Top-level concrete architecture and selected-subsystem hierarchy.
  • Responsibilities, important entities, and internal/external dependencies.
  • Low-level functions or entities responsible for major dependency edges.
  • Sequence or state analysis of important subsystem behavior.
  • Architectural style and noteworthy design patterns supported by evidence.
  • Conceptual comparison, alternatives, trade-offs, limitations, and lessons.
