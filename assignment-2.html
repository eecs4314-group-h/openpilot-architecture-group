Assignment brief

What must be delivered

The report and presentation should tell one evidence-backed story: how the team recovered the architecture, what the selected subsystem contains, how it behaves, and what the implementation reveals.

Assignment 2 analysis and supporting guidance

  • Extract the software architecture from the studied implementation with Understand.
  • Group top-level entities into subsystems and decompose the chosen subsystem further where useful.
  • Document the repeatable process used to map files and entities into the concrete architecture.
  • Show the selected subsystem's responsibilities, internal structure, and dependencies.
  • Identify the architectural style and any noteworthy design patterns supported by evidence.
  • Redraw tool output into clean diagrams that are readable in the report and on slides.
  • Use sequence or state diagrams to explain important behavior or interaction.
  • Document lessons and conclusions (Appendix A). Present modelling alternatives and trade-offs, and prepare to defend the findings (Appendix B recommendations).
  • Submit the Understand project file showing the component grouping (Section 6).
