Requirements & Comparison Method

Assignment 3: Discrepancy Analysis

  • Compare the conceptual architecture from A1 with the concrete architecture from A2.
  • Investigate and explain discrepancy rationales at the top subsystem level and the detailed Perception design level.
  • Propose and justify changes to the conceptual architecture, concrete architecture or both.
  • Show exactly which boundaries, responsibilities or dependencies would change in each view.

Source: EECS4314_Project_Description, Section 7, p.5.

Compare equivalent boundaries and relation types before classifying a difference. A change in naming or abstraction alone does not establish a discrepancy.

Lecture termMeaning in the comparison
ConvergencesRelations represented in both the conceptual and concrete views.
DivergencesRelations present in the concrete view but absent from the conceptual view.
AbsencesRelations expected in the conceptual view but absent from the concrete view.

Terminology: EECS 4314-6-ReflexionModels, slide 8. Each discrepancy record must distinguish the observed relation, its explanation and the proposed change.
